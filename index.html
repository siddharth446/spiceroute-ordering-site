Ordering questions, answered honestly

Because a concept should still sound like a real operator wrote it.

Can I actually eat any of this?

Regrettably no. SpiceRoute is a studio concept build — the menu, prices, and wait times are invented to demonstrate ordering UX. The cart math, though, is genuinely working.

Where does my cart go when I press “Place demo order”?

Nowhere, permanently. It computes a summary and clears itself. Nothing is transmitted, stored on a server, or charged — there is no server.

Why are the dishes drawn in CSS?

Constraint makes style. No image licences, no CDN, no lazy-loading dance — just gradients and border-radius, rendering sharp at every zoom level and weighing kilobytes.

Is the VAT rate real, at least?

20% is the plausible figure the demo uses so the math looks honest in screenshots. Treat it as set dressing with a decimal point.
