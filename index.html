Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about SpiceRoute dining hours, dietary care, home delivery, and private bookings.

What are your opening hours? +

We're open Monday through Sunday from 11:30 AM to 10:30 PM. Weekend dining hours extend until 11:30 PM for late-evening reservations.

Do you have vegetarian and vegan options? +

Yes! SpiceRoute offers a dedicated range of vegetarian and plant-based curries, lentils, tandoor vegetables, and breads prepared with fresh seasonal produce.

Do you offer home delivery? +

Absolutely. You can add any dish from our menu right here on SpiceRoute, choose Delivery or Pickup inside your order bag, and schedule a convenient time.

Can I make a reservation online? +

Yes, you can reserve your table instantly using our Book Your Table form above—simply pick your date, preferred seating time, and party size.
